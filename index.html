Why the problem exists

We can fly biology. We cannot observe every possibility.

RPM / CLINOSTAT

REAL FLIGHT

Target condition. Sparse, scarce and not interchangeable with analogues.

The data gap

What happens between measurements?

Many high-information assays are destructive. Flight observations are sparse. ORBIO connects those observations computationally while keeping uncertainty visible.

How ORBIO works

One experiment becomes a virtual organoid, then a forecast.

  1. Experiment: gravity, tissue, morphology, drug, dose, schedule, hardware.
  2. Virtual organoid: cells, O2, glucose, drug and mechanics evolve together.
  3. Cell graph: individual cells become nearby graph nodes.
  4. Organoid state: the graph compresses into a latent representation.
  5. Future prediction: day-1 state plus condition points to day-7 phenotype.

G_day1 -> z1 -> z7 -> phenotype

Where physics enters

Transport changes before biology answers.

The current simulator represents reaction-diffusion, sedimentation, finite medium and radiation. G-jitter and vibration-induced mixing are marked as next work, not current capability.
